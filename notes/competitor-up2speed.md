## Where we're clearly stronger

1. **Coverage.** They have one Mesa address. We have 20 venues statewide. For a student in
   Yuma, Tucson, Casa Grande or Wickenburg their $9 saving is gone before the freeway.
2. **Spanish.** Full second site + dedicated line + classes in Spanish, against one clause
   about a bilingual instructor. A whole market they've left open.
3. **Depth of information.** We publish the step-by-step day, check-in time, what to bring,
   payment methods, the four registration items, the waiver explained honestly, Zoom
   equipment, conduct rules, the childcare warning. They publish a bring-list and office hours.
4. **We publish dates; they publish none.** They say "classes are available to meet your
   schedule" and show no schedule anywhere. Their checkout takes $130 before the student
   knows when they're going.
5. **An hour earlier.** Our class ends 4:30, theirs 5:30.
6. **Their page hurts their own credibility.** "PRIVILIDGES" misspelled in the main headline
   paragraph; an unclosed parenthesis in the same sentence; Instagram and Twitter listed as
   follow links that link nowhere; `www.up2speedts.com` typed out **eleven times** in the body
   copy (keyword stuffing — a spam signal to search engines, not a ranking boost).

## Where they're genuinely beating us

1. **They built a campaign out of our $12 shipping fee.** Their words: "WE DO NOT ADD ANY
   CHARGES SUCH AS ADMIN OR SHIPPING FEES, FLAT $130.00" and "We do not advertise a fee and
   charge extra for Admin or Shipping." That is aimed at us. The comparison a shopper makes is
   $130 vs $151 — 16% more, not $9 more. Our own pages print "$12 for shipping" three times and
   never make it obvious that pickup costs nothing.
2. **They can take money at 11pm on a Sunday.** Card checkout on the site. Every one of our
   CTAs is call-us or the NSC tool. We lose everyone who won't phone — and people facing a
   suspension are exactly that population.
3. **Payment friction at the classroom too.** They take all major cards at check-in; we take
   exact cash (no change), money order, or a pre-arranged invoice.
4. **Third-party proof.** BBB A rating + Google Maps + Facebook, against nothing on our side.
   **Biggest exposure, cheapest fix.**
5. **They sell the outcome, we describe the process.** "CLEARS YOUR LICENSE WITH MVD, BY
   REMOVING THE ORDER TO SUSPEND" vs our "we will notify the Arizona MVD for you." Ours is more
   accurate; theirs is what the customer wants to hear.

## Their weak point — a self-contradiction on their own homepage

- Top of page: *"We also offer AZMVD Virtual Zoom Traffic Survival Classes at a $130 Flat Fee!"*
- Further down: *"Traffic Survival School or TSS, is an eight-hour class that is monitored by
  Arizona's Motor Vehicle Department (MVD) and must be taken in person—there isn't an online
  option."*

Both on the same site. We don't need to point at them — we just need to be unmistakably clear
and correct on the exact point where they're muddled.

## Recommended actions, in priority order

1. **Publish the 5 reviews we have and link Google.** Current plan waits for 10+. Worth
   reconsidering: 5 real reviews beats 0 while they show a BBB "A". Getting the Google Business
   Profile visible matters more than the count. *(Owner decision.)*
2. **Rewrite the price block to defuse the fee attack.** "$139. One price. No admin fee, no
   registration fee, no processing fee." Make workbook **pickup** the default presentation and
   shipping the footnote. Stronger option: absorb the $12 into the online price and advertise a
   true flat $139 — that's a margin call, not a copy call. *(Owner decision.)*
3. **Add a no-phone-call path.** Even a Google Form "request a seat." Real online payment is the
   bigger version of the same fix. (This is open item #3 in CLAUDE.md — now competitively urgent.)
4. **Local SEO + share previews.** They have a Google Maps place and a BBB backlink. We have 20
   locations and no `LocalBusiness`/`DrivingSchool` structured data, no Open Graph tags, no
   `hreflang` between EN and ES, no canonical. **Still blocked on: which domain is primary,
   `139aztss.com` or `aztraffic.school`?** No CNAME file in the repo, so the answer is in the
   GitHub Pages settings. (Open item #2 in CLAUDE.md — parked for the owner's son.)
5. **Add a "miss a class?" line.** They state a blunt "NO RETURN POLICY." We say nothing.
   "Miss your class? Call us and we'll reschedule you" is warmer and costs nothing.

## Bottom line

A single-location Mesa school with a typo-ridden page, competing on nine dollars and a card
reader. We're a statewide, bilingual, genuinely informative operation that hasn't switched on
its proof or its checkout. Fix 1 and 2 and the nine dollars stops mattering.
